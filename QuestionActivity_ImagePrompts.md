# Workbook Image Prompts — New Question-Word Pages

Prompts for **new** workbook pages to add after the existing 30 pages. They teach the question words used in the Ask & Answer activity (なに / だれ / どこ / いつ / なぜ / どうして / どう / どれ / いくら / いくつ / なんじ / ですか) and match the visual style of the current `WorkbookPages_Correct/` images (e.g. `17_DaysOfTheWeek.png`, `20_Practice_FillInBlanks.png`).

Suggested filenames: `31_QuestionWords_Vocabulary.png` … `43_QuestionWords_FillInPractice.png` (the Ask & Answer practice is now an interactive web activity at `voicepractice.html` instead of a printed page). Pages alternate between the two existing layouts:

- **Vocabulary layout** (like `17_DaysOfTheWeek.png`): sky-blue background, sun + moon corner icons, banner title in large hiragana with romaji subtitle, centered vocabulary table with colored emoji dots, a green "Example:" pill at the bottom showing one sample sentence with a chibi child illustration.
- **Practice layout** (like `20_Practice_FillInBlanks.png`): cream/pink pastel background with dotted-line paper texture, scalloped blue top border, "れんしゅう / Renshū / Practice" header, kawaii bear / bunny / chick / puppy mascots peeking from the margins, pastel rounded text-box rows with fill-in-the-blank underlines, small music notes and stars confetti, thin blue scalloped bottom border.

---

## Shared Style Header (prepend to every prompt)

> Children's Japanese workbook page illustration, portrait 2:3 aspect, kawaii style, matte flat colors with soft outlines, printable-textbook look on lightly textured paper. Kana must be rendered accurately and legibly as typed; romaji gloss in a small clean sans-serif under every Japanese line; English translation in a slightly smaller secondary color. Use the exact layout described (Vocabulary or Practice). No photorealism, no 3D, no glossy effects, no extra decorative text beyond what is specified, no watermarks.

## Page 32 — なに (Nani) — What

**Layout:** Vocabulary. **Title banner:** **「なに」**, romaji **Nani**, subtitle **What**. Icon: magnifying glass with sparkles. Vocabulary table of **common "nani" questions** (not new words, complete sentences):

- これは なに ですか？ — Kore wa nani desu ka? — What is this?
- なにが すき ですか？ — Nani ga suki desu ka? — What do you like?
- なにを たべますか？ — Nani wo tabemasu ka? — What will you eat?
- おなまえは なに ですか？ — Onamae wa nani desu ka? — What is your name?
- なにいろ ですか？ — Nani iro desu ka? — What color is it?

**Example pill:** a chibi girl pointing at a red apple on a plate; speech bubble **「これは なに ですか？」** and reply bubble **「りんご です。」** (Ringo desu. / It's an apple.).

## Page 42 — ですか: Making Questions

**Layout:** Vocabulary. **Title banner:** **「ですか」**, romaji **Desu ka**, subtitle **Making Questions**. Icon: a big cheerful pink "?". **Top note box** (yellow sticky-note style): "Add **か** to the end of a sentence to make it a question. **です → ですか**." Table of **statement → question** pairs:

- ねこ です。 → ねこ ですか？ — It is a cat. → Is it a cat?
- すき です。 → すき ですか？ — I like it. → Do you like it?
- げんき です。 → げんき ですか？ — I'm well. → Are you well?
- あした です。 → あした ですか？ — It is tomorrow. → Is it tomorrow?

**Example pill:** two chibi kids face each other, one with a flat "." speech bubble and the other with a big "?" bubble; underneath, a simple arrow diagram **「です」 + 「か」 → 「ですか？」** drawn in kid-crayon style.
