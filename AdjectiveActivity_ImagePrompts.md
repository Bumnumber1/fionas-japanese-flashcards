# Workbook Image Prompts — Adjective Sentence Pages

Prompts for **new** workbook pages teaching how to use adjectives in a sentence. They pair with the new web activity **Sentence Builder** (`sentencebuilder.html`) and the separated vocab sections (**い-Adjectives**, **な-Adjectives**, **Colors**) on the main flashcards page.

Suggested filenames continue after the existing set (which ends at `47_EverydayPhrases.png`):

- `48_iAdjectives_InASentence.png`
- `49_naAdjectives_InASentence.png`
- `50_Colors_InASentence.png`

All three use the **Practice layout** (like `20_Practice_FillInBlanks.png` / `43_QuestionWords_Practice.png`): cream/pink pastel background with dotted-line paper texture, scalloped blue top border, a header banner, kawaii bear / bunny / chick / puppy mascots peeking from the margins, pastel rounded text-box rows with fill-in-the-blank underlines, small music notes and stars confetti, a thin blue scalloped bottom border, and a cloud-shaped word bank near the bottom.

Each page also has a **"How it works" pattern strip** near the top: four little color-coded word boxes shown left-to-right in Japanese order — **thing → は → describer → です** — with a tiny English gloss under each box, so kids see the meaning of every word while keeping Japanese order.

## Page 48 — い-Adjectives in a Sentence

**Layout:** Practice. **Header banner:** **「いけいようし」** with romaji **i-keiyōshi** and English subtitle **い-Adjectives in a Sentence**. Header accent color: teal/green. Mascot: a smiling green frog in the top-left and a kawaii bear in the top-right.

**Pattern strip (top), four color-coded boxes left→right in Japanese order:**

| Box 1 (blue) | Box 2 (gray) | Box 3 (teal) | Box 4 (green) |
|---|---|---|---|
| いぬ | は | おおきい | です |
| inu | wa | ookii | desu |
| dog | (topic) | big | is |

Caption under the strip: **「いぬは おおきい です。」 = "The dog is big."** Small note tag (teal sticky-note): "An **い-adjective** goes right before **です**. No extra word needed!"

**Mini vocabulary reminder row** (small emoji + word, no blanks): おおきい (big), ちいさい (small), おいしい (delicious), あかい (red), あおい (blue), うれしい (happy), ねむい (sleepy).

**Five fill-in-the-blank practice rows** (blank drawn as a long underline inside a tinted box), each with a hint icon on the right matching the answer:

1. Pink box — **「ケーキは ＿＿＿ です。」** — *Keeki wa ____ desu.* — "The cake is ____." Hint icon: a slice of cake with sparkles (answer: おいしい / delicious).
2. Yellow box — **「いぬは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "The dog is ____." Hint: a big elephant (answer: おおきい / big).
3. Blue box — **「そらは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "The sky is ____." Hint: a blue sky (answer: あおい / blue).
4. Green box — **「りんごは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "The apple is ____." Hint: a red apple (answer: あかい / red).
5. Lavender box — **「ねこは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "The cat is ____." Hint: a tiny mouse (answer: ちいさい / small).

**Bottom word bank** (cloud-shaped callout): **おおきい ・ ちいさい ・ おいしい ・ あおい ・ あかい**. Musical notes and pastel stars scattered in the margins; chick peeking from the bottom-left, puppy from the bottom-right.

## Page 49 — な-Adjectives in a Sentence

**Layout:** Practice. **Header banner:** **「なけいようし」** with romaji **na-keiyōshi** and English subtitle **な-Adjectives in a Sentence**. Header accent color: purple/lavender. Mascot: a kawaii purple bunny top-left, a smiling flower-headed chibi top-right.

**Pattern strip (top), four color-coded boxes left→right in Japanese order:**

| Box 1 (blue) | Box 2 (gray) | Box 3 (purple) | Box 4 (green) |
|---|---|---|---|
| はな | は | きれい | です |
| hana | wa | kirei | desu |
| flower | (topic) | pretty | is |

Caption under the strip: **「はなは きれい です。」 = "The flower is pretty."** Two note tags (purple sticky-notes):
- "After **です**, use the word with **no な**: きれい です ✅."
- "But to describe a noun directly, add **な**: きれいな はな = a pretty flower. (きれい ends in い but is still a な-adjective!)"

**Mini vocabulary reminder row:** すき (like / favorite), きらい (dislike), げんき (well / healthy), きれい (pretty).

**Five fill-in-the-blank practice rows:**

1. Pink box — **「ねこは ＿＿＿ です。」** — *Neko wa ____ desu.* — "I like the cat. / The cat is my favorite." Hint icon: heart (answer: すき / liked).
2. Yellow box — **「はなは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "The flower is ____." Hint: cherry blossom (answer: きれい / pretty).
3. Blue box — **「わたしは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "I am ____." Hint: flexed arm (answer: げんき / well).
4. Green box — **「ブロッコリーは ＿＿＿ です。」** — "I dislike broccoli." Hint: a sad face by broccoli (answer: きらい / disliked).
5. Lavender box (describe-a-noun bonus) — **「＿＿＿な はな」** — "a ____ flower." Hint: a sparkly pretty flower (answer: きれい → きれいな). Little arrow reminder **きれい + な → きれいな**.

**Bottom word bank** (cloud-shaped callout): **すき ・ きらい ・ げんき ・ きれい**. Music notes and stars in the margins; bear peeking bottom-left, chick bottom-right.

## Page 50 — Colors (いろ)

**Layout:** Vocabulary (a colorful color chart — *not* a fill-in page). **Header banner:** **「いろ」** with romaji **iro** and English subtitle **Colors**. Header accent: a rainbow arc. Mascot: a kawaii paint-palette character top-left, a puppy holding a crayon top-right. A little rainbow and scattered stars/music notes in the margins.

**Main color chart** — one row per color, each row showing a **big filled color swatch (circle or crayon)** on the left, then the Japanese word, romaji, and English. Show **all eleven** colors together so it works as a single color reference:

| Swatch | Hiragana / Katakana | Romaji | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| red circle | あかい | akai | red |
| blue circle | あおい | aoi | blue |
| yellow circle | きいろい | kiiroi | yellow |
| white circle (with thin outline) | しろい | shiroi | white |
| black circle | くろい | kuroi | black |
| brown circle | ちゃいろい | chairoi | brown |
| green circle | みどり | midori | green |
| purple circle | むらさき | murasaki | purple |
| pink circle | ピンク | pinku | pink |
| orange circle | オレンジ | orenji | orange |
| gray circle | はいいろ | haiiro | gray |

Put a small **「い」 tag** beside the first six (the い-adjective colors: あかい–ちゃいろい) and a small **「noun」 tag** beside the last five (みどり–はいいろ) so the two types are visually distinguished.

**Small explanation box at the bottom** (amber sticky-note / rounded callout — keep it short and kid-friendly):

> **How the colors work**
> Most of these colors (**あかい, あおい, きいろい, しろい, くろい, ちゃいろい**) are **い-adjectives** — you can stick them right onto a thing: **あかい りんご** = "a red apple."
> But **みどり, むらさき, ピンク, オレンジ, はいいろ** are **nouns**, not adjectives. To put a noun-color in front of a thing, add **の**: **みどりの くるま** = "a green car."
> Either way, to say *"X is [color]"* just use **です**: **そらは あおい です** ("The sky is blue") / **くるまは みどり です** ("The car is green").
