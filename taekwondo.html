WELCOME TO THE DOJO

Your learning is your superpower.

  1. Choose a move. All seven basics are ready at white belt. Each new belt earns one stronger special. Picking a special gives you a harder activity, shown with its extra damage. Changing basic moves keeps the same question. Each special keeps its own challenge when you switch away and back. Hints and writing lock your choice until you answer; completed basics cannot become finishers afterward.
  2. Speak to fight. Reading rounds require the microphone: pronounce the hiragana, katakana, or word in Japanese. Allow microphone access when your browser asks. No recognised speech or a microphone error costs no energy.
  3. Block the counter. A mistake breaks your streak and queues a rival counter. Your next activity is a block chance: correct prevents all its damage; incorrect lets that counter land. Then you return to attacking. The final opponent, Master Steif, has short grey hair and a special rule: each mistake deals 33.3% of your full energy immediately, with no block chance. Three mistakes end his match.
  4. Hands-free flow. In reading rounds, tap Start hands-free once and allow the microphone. Say each new prompt as the mic starts automatically. Fast, clean attacks earn +6 damage within two seconds or +3 within four seconds, with speed callouts and brighter combos. Mistakes still offer a block chance against ordinary rivals. Stop hands-free, Pause, or leave the page to stop listening. Silence costs no energy; the mic pauses after two quiet retries.
  5. Find your rhythm. Clean attack answers grow damage; every third triggers a combo. Guided attacks deal half damage and earn no streak. Correct guided blocks still stop the counter. There is no timer.
  6. Earn your belts. Rank changes only after you defeat a rival. Two belts each teach hiragana reading, katakana reading, hiragana writing, and katakana writing. The first belt of each pair covers 23 characters; the second covers all 46. High belts read words aloud; black belt writes words with kanji.
  7. Collect six treats. Finish hiragana reading, katakana reading, hiragana writing, katakana writing, word reading, and kanji writing. Each section earns its own ice cream, with its flavour saved on this device.

Draw with a finger, pen, or mouse. KanjiVG checks stroke shape, order, and direction. Use Tab and Enter for buttons.
