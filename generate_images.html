🌊 Ocean Image Generator

Click through each prompt to generate images in Bing Image Creator

📋 How to use:

  1. Click "Copy Prompt" to copy the text
  2. Click "Open Bing Creator" to open Bing Image Creator
  3. Paste the prompt and click Create
  4. Download the best result, rename it to the filename shown
  5. Put it in C:\JapaneseLessons\ocean_images\
  6. Click "Mark Done & Next" to move on

💡 Tip: For transparency, after downloading, go to remove.bg to remove the background.
